# Precision / Truth Boundary

Precision answers: **which evidence is most relevant to the active intent?**

Truth answers: **what temporal/epistemic classification does that evidence support?**

Precision may rank CURRENT, HISTORICAL, SUPERSEDED, CONTRADICTED, UNCERTAIN and UNRESOLVED candidates. It must preserve those supplied classifications for Truth to evaluate. It may not transform HISTORICAL evidence into a CURRENT claim, promote DERIVED/INFERRED evidence, or settle conflicting evidence.

The Truth-compatible output therefore preserves candidate identity, Artifact Reference, source revisions, authority class, truth status, provenance, reason codes and ranking scores.

Ranking score is retrieval metadata, not truth confidence.
