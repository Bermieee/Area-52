## Working rules

1. Build against the canonical cognitive blueprint.
2. Do not silently redefine architecture through implementation.
3. Keep source evidence separate from learned state.
4. Preserve provenance and temporal history.
5. Models propose; canonical owners settle.
6. Keep deep study/background cognition outside foreground generation where possible.
7. Add benchmark coverage with each cognitive subsystem.
8. Prefer replaceable interfaces over framework lock-in.
9. External frameworks are references/adapters unless explicitly adopted.
10. Do not migrate authority into production Nexus until shadow evaluation passes.
